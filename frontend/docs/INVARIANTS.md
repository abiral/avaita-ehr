# Invariants and the tests that cover them

Every invariant in `design-files/docs/codebase.md` section 1, with the automated tests that prove it. Unit and component tests run in `npm run check`; e2e tests run in `npm run e2e` and in CI.

## Access and session

| Invariant                                                                                                 | Tests                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One sign-in screen with a Patient or Staff toggle; staff land on C1 unless they have exactly one facility | `src/features/access.test.tsx`: "signs a patient in and lands on the portal (or the safe returnTo)", "sends staff with several facilities to the workspace picker, and shows SSO for staff only", "sends staff with one facility straight to the workspace"; `src/domain/identity/session.test.ts`: "asks for a facility unless exactly one membership" |
| Staff lock after 10 minutes, patients after 15; the lock keeps the URL; drafts survive in memory          | `src/app/app.test.tsx`: "locks staff after 10 minutes, keeps the URL, unlocks with the password", "locks patients after 15 minutes and signs out after 30 minutes locked, clearing the cache"; `src/app/invariants.test.tsx`: "session: unsaved form text survives the idle lock in memory"                                                             |
| Any 401 returns to sign in (or the lock screen) with `returnTo`                                           | `src/infrastructure/http/http.test.ts`: "emits sessionEnded on 401 and sessionLocked on 423"; `src/app/app.test.tsx`: "redirects signed-out users to sign in with returnTo"; `src/app/guards.test.ts`: "sends signed-out users to sign in with returnTo"; e2e `flow1-access.spec.ts`: "a protected link opens after sign in (returnTo)"                 |
| Sign out, the 30 minute locked timeout and facility switch clear the query cache and drafts               | `src/app/invariants.test.tsx`: "session: signing out clears the whole query cache and every in-memory draft"; `src/app/app.test.tsx`: "locks patients after 15 minutes and signs out after 30 minutes locked, clearing the cache", "clears the cache and drafts, and later requests carry the new facility header"                                      |
